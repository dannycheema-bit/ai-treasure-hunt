## Our read
- The narrator's numbers are accurate; the title is not.
- **Tension with his own buying:** on 5 Nov he says Micron will never get back to a 10x P/E and that all P/Es are coming
  down. In the same week he made Micron his largest purchase (8 Nov weekly). Both can hold (cheap and getting cheaper may
  still rally), but the low P/E is his warning, not his reason.
- Bitcoin's 200-day (~$73.1k) is the level his wave count needs. It closed at $73,720 on 10 Nov. The narrator's own failure
  test is a close well below it.
