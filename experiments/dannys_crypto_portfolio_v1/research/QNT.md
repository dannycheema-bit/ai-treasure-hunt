# Quant (QNT): study first, then an independent view

Compiled 2026-10-13 from public sources, including Quant Network Ltd's **audited accounts filed at
Companies House**. Educational research, not advice.

## Where it sits
- **Danny's Crypto Portfolio v1:** held. It is the **only holding not in Visser's 46-name index**, so no
  index view supports it.
- **Jev usage run (13 Oct):** P(revenue/usage +25% by Mar-2027) was not assessed, because QNT is outside
  the 46.

## 1. Understand the business
- **Overledger** is an interoperability gateway. Banks and central banks use one regulated API to work
  across many blockchains: tokenized deposits, programmable money, connections to payment rails.
- **Main mandates:**
  - **The Clearing House** (the settlement utility owned by the large US banks) chose Quant on
    **24 Sep 2026** for its On-Chain Money / tokenized-deposit network. It is reportedly backed by
    25 large US institutions, connects to the RTP and CHIPS payment rails, and is **due to open to
    participants in H1 2027**.
  - **ECB digital euro:** "pioneer partner" for the innovation platform since May 2025. That is testing,
    not production.
  - **Bank of England / digital pound:** Project Rosalind (programmability tests).
  - **Murex:** partnership since Mar 2026, embedding tokenized deposits into the MX.3 trading and
    post-trade platform used by many banks.
  - **Fusion Rollup:** mainnet since Jun 2026, said to connect 74 networks.
  - Claims such as a UK bank consortium (HSBC/Barclays/Lloyds) and an "ECB Overledger trial" come from
    social posts or video titles and were **not verified**.

## 2. Measure what actually happens: the audited accounts
Quant Network Ltd (company no. 09798383), full audited accounts for the year to **30 Sep 2025**, filed
6 Jul 2026, auditor MAH:

| | FY2025 | FY2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue (all "licence fees and service agreements", **all UK**) | **£11.52m** | £10.70m (+7.6%) |
| Gross profit | £9.76m (84.8% margin) | £9.15m |
| Operating profit | £1.79m | £2.07m |
| Profit before tax | £1.92m | £2.09m |
| Cash | £7.87m | £5.31m |
| Headcount | 44 | 43 |
| R&D expense | £0.45m | £1.25m |

- **Small, profitable and growing slowly.** The director names **"commercial and revenue concentration
  risk: a significant proportion of revenue is derived from a small number of client relationships."**
- These accounts **predate** the Clearing House mandate, so they are a clean "before" baseline.
- **There are no QNT or crypto assets on this company's balance sheet** (total assets £9.18m: fixed assets,
  debtors and cash). Revenue is recognised in pounds. Any token treasury sits elsewhere in the group: the
  parent is Quant Network Holdings Ltd, and the ultimate parent is Quant Holdings Limited.

## 3. Follow the token
| Point | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Supply | Maximum 14.61m QNT; CoinGecko shows ~14.56m circulating (99.6%). **No unlock overhang**, unlike LINK and ZRO. |
| Licence model | Overledger licences are described as payable in QNT, with tokens locked in the Quant Treasury for about 12 months. |
| But | An independent 8Blocks audit says clients **can pay in stablecoins**, with Quant locking equivalent QNT from its own treasury; Quant **does not publish** how many tokens are locked against licences. |
| Concentration | 8Blocks reports ~9.55m QNT (~65%) held in a smart contract / treasury that the company controls. That conflicts with "99.6% circulating", so the free float is unclear. |

**The gap consensus skips:** the bank wins accrue to **a private UK company that earns pounds**. Token
holders have no claim on that equity or revenue. Their link is the licence-lock mechanism, which is
company-controlled and undisclosed.

## 4. Valuation, roughly
- QNT market cap is about **$3.14bn** (~£2.3bn) at $216: **+235% in 30 days**, +130% over a year, and still
  about 50% below the 2021 high of $427.
- That is about **200× the operating company's revenue** of £11.5m. The comparison is not like for like,
  because the token does not own the revenue, but it shows how much future adoption the price assumes.

## 5. Independent view (formed before reading price targets)
- **The business is real, profitable and now has its biggest validation yet.** The Clearing House mandate
  puts Overledger inside US bank infrastructure. That matters more than the ECB "pioneer" tag.
- **Revenue so far is modest:** £11.5m, +7.6%, 44 people, concentrated clients. The Clearing House network
  only opens in H1 2027, so **any revenue inflection is at least 9–12 months away** and will show up in the
  FY2026 and FY2027 accounts.
- **The token tripled in a month on the announcement.** Consensus has priced the transformation before any
  revenue exists.
- **Its structure is the reverse of LINK and ZRO.** QNT has **no supply overhang**: a fixed, almost fully
  circulating supply. But its **value-capture link is opaque and company-controlled**. LINK and ZRO
  publish buybacks or burns on-chain; QNT does not publish lock data.
- **The variable that decides it:** whether bank adoption (Clearing House go-live, Murex distribution) turns
  into **verifiable QNT locked or bought for licences**, rather than pound revenue at the company alone.

## 6. Measurable tests (set now)
**Would strengthen the case:**
- **FY2026 accounts** (year to 30 Sep 2026, due by about Jul 2027) show revenue growth of at least 30%.
- The Clearing House network goes live in H1 2027 with named participating banks transacting.
- Quant **publishes licence-lock data**, or the treasury contract's balance visibly rises with new clients
  (track the contract on-chain).
- Revenue concentration falls (more named production clients).

**Would weaken it:**
- FY2026 revenue growth below 10%.
- The Clearing House go-live slips past H1 2027.
- The treasury contract releases large amounts of QNT to exchanges.
- Disclosure confirms that clients pay mainly in fiat or stablecoins with no market QNT purchases.
