## Main message
Monday's fall was the catalyst that turned AI believers bearish. The trigger was Jensen Huang's ouster, which raised
doubts about "the bank of Nvidia" and its deals. He does **not** think this is "it", but QQQ is below its 200-day, so
he is **neutral, with no long trading positions** until it is back above. He still holds AI names he expects to at least
double in a year.

He sees no recession: nominal GDP, earnings forecasts, Redbook sales and jobless claims are all strong. Instead he
calls it a **liquidity event like late 2018**, driven by rising real 2-year yields since May (Warsh's hawkish Fed).
His 2018 parallels: QQQ below the 200-day on 16 Nov in both years; Brent falling from October.

Credit is the best sign: leveraged loans are near all-time highs while stocks fall. The turn needs falling real
2-year yields, a weaker dollar and beta beating quality. The dollar, quality vs beta and inverted Bitcoin are "all one
trade".
