## Main message
**V or U?** The S&P is 12% off its high after one of its fastest falls in decades. He bets on a **V**: a sharp fall,
then a fast rally to new highs within a year. A U means a recession with falling earnings and job losses, which he
does not see.

His reasons:
- earnings growth is still expected next year;
- capital raising has probably peaked (Oracle says it is done; Anthropic needs none for now);
- the drags were liquidity (Fed, real 2-year yields, oil, capital raises), not the economy.

The closest analogue is 2018, so the question is whether the Fed pivots. On his history, a pivot means "hike in
December and signal that's enough". Watch the dollar: a falling dollar should coincide with the low. Bitcoin is "the
same thing", all lockstep with liquidity.

  he quotes.

  (15 Nov), "be ready" (22 Nov). From 6 Nov to 20 Nov the S&P fell a further 6.3%.
