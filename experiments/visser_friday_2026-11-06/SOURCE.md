## Our read
- The scale claims check out: this was a historic day for tech and volatility. The cause (forced selling, not
  fundamentals) is his interpretation, built mostly on bank notes and a Grok summary of posts on X.
- **The 3 Nov "bottom" call did not hold:** SMH fell another 14% in two days after he said sentiment had bottomed. The
  VIX-outside-Bollinger signal from 2 Nov was followed by the largest VIX spike since 2018 within three days. That is one
  data point against the recipe, not a test of it.
- He is an interested party: he is buying, and he says so.
- Crypto was not discussed. BTC fell from $80.4k to $75.9k on 5 Nov, below the ~$82k trigger he gave on 5 Nov.
